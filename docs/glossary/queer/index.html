Queer

Queer theory is concerned with sexualities which do not conform to heterosexual norms. In this sense, queer sexuality encompasses a vast range of desires and practices. Queer theory focuses on the socially constructed nature of sexuality and examines how desires are channeled into socially acceptable forms. At its most radical, queer theory considers each individual’s sexuality to be different and claims therefore that one way or another, we are all queer.
